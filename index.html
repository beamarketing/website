FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

CABR is Beamr's patented technology that analyzes video content frame-by-frame and optimizes encoding parameters in real-time. It achieves up to 50% file size reduction while maintaining mathematically proven visual quality.
Beamr uses perceptual quality metrics and a unique quality-assured encoding approach. Every encoded frame is verified against the original to ensure no perceptible quality loss, guaranteed by mathematical proof.
Beamr supports all major codecs including H.264/AVC, H.265/HEVC, AV1, and VP9. It works with standard container formats like MP4, MKV, and TS, at resolutions up to 8K.
Most customers are up and running within days. Beamr provides drop-in compatibility with major encoding pipelines, CDNs, and cloud platforms. Our team provides dedicated integration support.
